Made in India, for India

{{ app.app_name|default:"EduChats" }} is designed, built and run in India for Indian users. It is created, maintained and owned by EduTrellis Private Limited, an Indian company, and works under Indian law.

Your data is safe with us

Your messages, photos and account details travel over secure HTTPS connections, only you and the people you chat with can open your chats, and the system is backed up regularly. We never sell your personal data or share it with advertisers.

What we keep

  • The account details you give us: name, email, phone, state and city.
  • What you send: messages, photos, videos, files and locations you choose to share.
  • Basic technical details (IP address, device, last seen) to keep accounts safe and stop misuse.

Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to show, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw your consent, and raise a complaint. Use ⋮ → Privacy to control what others see about you, and Maps to choose what shows on the map.

Complaints and grievances

For any complaint about your data, a chat or another user, contact our grievance team. We aim to reply within 24 hours and settle complaints within 15 days.
