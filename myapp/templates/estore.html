A gadget store run by a tech company

EduTrellis Private Limited has been working since 2020 — building and selling websites, and running the technical services around them: hosting, SEO, digital marketing and Google Business, for clients across India from our base in Lucknow.

Along the way we bought a lot of gear for our own team and for clients, and got tired of spec sheets that didn't match reality. So this year we launched the store. Everything listed here is stock we keep, unbox and test before it ships, sold at a fixed price with the warranty intact.

What you get with every order

  • Sealed, genuine units with brand warranty intact
  • Specs listed honestly — real battery and charging numbers
  • Dispatch within 24 hours, tracking sent on WhatsApp
  • 7-day returns and a human who answers your messages
  • GST invoice on request for business purchases
