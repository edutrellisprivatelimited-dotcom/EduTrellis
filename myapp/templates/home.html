Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

What is the ₹2,499/month Website Management plan?

It's our flagship monthly plan where we take complete ownership of your website and digital presence. We handle logo/banner updates, content uploads, SEO, social media, digital marketing on Instagram, Facebook & Google, and assign a dedicated website manager — so you focus 100% on your core work.

What does "3 Months in the First Month" mean?

As a special welcome offer, your first billing cycle gives you 3 months of access instead of the usual 1 month. This gives you ample time to see real results before your next billing cycle begins — it's our way of showing confidence in our service.

How long does it take to build my website?

Static websites are typically delivered within 3–5 business days. Dynamic/e-commerce websites take 10–15 days depending on complexity. Custom large projects are discussed and planned during consultation, with realistic timelines provided upfront.

Do I need a website already to use the management plan?

No! The management plan works even if your business is currently inactive or you don't have a website yet. We'll help set everything up from scratch — website, social media handles, Google Business profile and more — all within the plan.

What platforms do you work with?

We work with all major platforms including Classplus, Apex, EduGorilla, WordPress, custom-built websites, Shopify, WooCommerce and more. During your free consultation, tell us your current platform and we'll tailor our services accordingly.

Is there a contract or lock-in period?

For the monthly plan (₹2,499/month), there's no long-term lock-in — you can cancel anytime after the first month. The 6-month Complete Manager Package is a fixed 6-month commitment at a discounted rate. One-time website projects have no recurring obligations.
