Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our website management, SEO, Meta Ads & digital services

What is the ₹2,999/month Website Management plan?

It's our flagship monthly plan where we take complete ownership of your website and digital presence. We handle logo/banner updates, content uploads, SEO, social media, Meta Ads & digital marketing on Instagram, Facebook & Google, and assign a dedicated website manager — so you focus 100% on your core work.

Does EduTrellis offer WordPress management and optimization?

Yes! We provide complete WordPress management and optimization services including plugin updates, speed optimization, security hardening, content management, and SEO optimization for coaching institutes and businesses across India.

How long does it take to build my website?

Static websites are typically delivered within 3–5 business days. Dynamic/e-commerce websites take 10–15 days depending on complexity. Custom large projects and Android app development are discussed and planned during consultation, with realistic timelines provided upfront.

Do I need a website already to use the management plan?

No! The management plan works even if your business is currently inactive or you don't have a website yet. We'll help set everything up from scratch — website, social media handles, Google Business profile and more — all within the plan.

Does EduTrellis run Meta Ads and Google Ads?

Yes, we manage Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) and Google Ads as part of our digital marketing services. We handle ad creation, targeting, budget management, and reporting to maximize your ROI for businesses and coaching institutes across India.

Is there a contract or lock-in period?

For the monthly plan (₹2,999/month), there's no long-term lock-in — you can cancel anytime after the first month. The 6-month Complete Manager Package is a fixed 6-month commitment at a discounted rate. One-time website projects have no recurring obligations.
