Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

What is the ₹2,999/month Website Management plan?

Our flagship monthly plan where we take complete ownership of your website and digital presence — logo/banner updates, content uploads, SEO, social media, digital marketing on Instagram, Facebook & Google, and a dedicated website manager assigned to you.

What is the "1 Month + 15 Days FREE" first enroll offer?

When you enroll for the first time, you get 1 month + 15 extra days of access for the price of 1 month. It's our welcome bonus — extra time to see real results before your next billing cycle.

How long does it take to build my website?

Static websites: 3–5 business days. Dynamic/e-commerce websites: 10–15 days. Custom large projects: timeline discussed during consultation.

Do I need a website already to use the management plan?

No! We'll set everything up from scratch — website, social media handles, Google Business profile and more — all within the plan.

What platforms do you work with?

Classplus, Apex, EduGorilla, WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, custom-built websites and more. Tell us your platform during the free consultation and we'll tailor accordingly.

Is there a contract or lock-in period?

For the monthly plan (₹2,999/month), there's no long-term lock-in — you can cancel anytime after the first month. The 6-month Complete Manager Package is a fixed 6-month commitment at a discounted rate. One-time website projects have no recurring obligations.
